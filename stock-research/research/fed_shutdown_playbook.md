[Newstribune 10/29](https://www.newstribune.com/news/2026/oct/29/wall-street-ends-higher-as-fed-holds-key-rate), [investinglive 10/28](https://investinglive.com/central-banks/recap-fed-holds-at-3-75-4-in-10-2-vote-as-cook-dissents-for-a-hike-december-move-in-focus/)
- **2022–23 (memory):** 11 hike days averaged roughly +0.3% but with ±1.5–3% swings; day-2 reversals were common (May 5, 2022 −3.6%; Jun 16, 2022 −3.3%). IBKR: S&P lower over the following 3 days after 5 of 6 meetings; SIFMA: no consistent pattern. [SIFMA 4/2023](https://www.sifma.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/SIFMA-Insights-Spotlight-April-2023-Market-Metrics.pdf)
- **Dec 19, 2018 (memory):** fully priced hike + hawkish presser: S&P −1.5% that day, about −6% more over the next 3 sessions (coinciding with a shutdown start), then a sharp recovery.
- **Sectors (memory, general pattern):** hawkish outcomes hit semis/high-beta growth hardest (MU typically 2–3x the index move); off-price/defensive retail (TJX) holds best; mega-cap AMZN in between.
- **Takeaway:** a priced-in hike is rarely the problem; the press conference and day 2 are. I could not compute a clean "next 5 days" average; no source gave one.
