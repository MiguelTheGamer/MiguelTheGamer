## 5. Verdict: MU, conditionally; otherwise neither. Skip AVGO.

**AVGO:** reporting after the close on Fed day stacks two binary events in one evening, with Oracle possibly the same night. For a $500 swing the outcome is close to a coin flip. Only reasonable as a Dec 1–8 trade exited fully before the Dec 9 close.

**MU:** it has the cleaner setup. It is 30%+ off highs, DRAM is still tight, Citi is supportive, and a pre-earnings run-up is plausible. Against that, the second derivative on pricing is negative (NAND falling, server DRAM about +1%), and a 19% one-day bounce in a falling market is typical bear-rally behaviour.

**Rules**
1. **Entry:** Dec 1–8 only, limit order at $650–700. Prefer buying after the Dec 3 hearing (Dec 4–8). Do not chase above $700. $500 buys roughly 0.7 share, so the broker must support fractional shares.
2. **No trade if** MU closes below ~$600 (under the Nov 18 low near $605), NVDA gives back more than half of Monday's gain on investigation news, or Dell/Marvell flag AI order pushouts.
3. **Stop:** daily close below $600, about −10 to −14% or $50–70.
4. **Exit:** sell half at +10–12% or by Dec 16 close, whichever comes first. Sell everything by Dec 16 if the position is not green. Consider being fully out or at half size before the Dec 9 close.
5. **Hold through earnings:** at most the remaining half (~$250), accepting a ±15% move.
6. If the $650–700 pullback does not come, the answer is **neither**. Do not swap into AVGO as a consolation trade.
