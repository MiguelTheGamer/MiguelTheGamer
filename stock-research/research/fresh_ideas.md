## 1. H&R Block (HRB), ~$6B cap
- **Catalyst:** Investor Day Dec 3, 8:30am ET, New York; CEO and CFO on long-term strategy (company release Nov 2).
- **Why:** Tax prep demand ignores the Fed and shutdowns. Stock is down ~17% from $55 three weeks ago. Q1 (Nov 9) reaffirmed FY27 EPS of $6.04–6.24, about 7.5x earnings. The company bought back $100M at $45.68, right at today's price, with ~$500M authorization left.
- **Risk:** Long-term targets disappoint, or AI/free-filing fears keep the multiple low. The stock has fallen for eight straight sessions.
- **Plan:** Buy Nov 24–25 at ≤$47. Stop $42.50. Sell by Dec 4 close; take it early at $50+.

## 2. Nu Holdings (NU), ~$57B cap
- **Catalyst:** First Investor Day Dec 8, New York (announced Sep 2).
- **Why:** Sitting on its 52-week low after Lula's Oct 25 runoff win hit Brazilian assets, and after confirmed-then-stalled Monzo talks. Q3 (Nov 12) added 3.7M customers. UBS still targets $17.80, Needham $19.
- **Risk:** This is not defensive: Brazil politics, the real, and a Nov 20 downgrade. The event is the day before the Dec 9 FOMC.
- **Plan:** Buy Nov 24–25 at ≤$12.20. Stop $10.60. Sell Dec 8 before the close, whatever happens; target $13.20.

## 3. Becton Dickinson (BDX), ~$61B cap
- **Catalyst:** Investor Day Dec 10, 8:30am ET; a new long-range plan is expected (secondhand, from a conference summary).
- **Why:** Defensive medical supplies. FY27 EPS guide of $13.10–13.30 (Nov 5) puts it near 16x. Stock rose ~8% after earnings and has held.
- **Risk:** Already near its high, so good news may be priced. Dec 10 is also CPI day, one day before the funding deadline.
- **Plan:** Buy by Dec 4 at ≤$217 (your plan bars buys Dec 9–11). Stop $203. Sell Dec 10 close; target $225.

## 4. Progressive (PGR), ~$135B cap
- **Catalyst:** Annual variable dividend. Last year it was $13.50, declared Dec 5 and announced Dec 8. November results came Dec 17 last year. Neither 2026 date is published yet.
- **Why:** This is the one true category-(a) fit: insurers earn more on their float when rates rise, and auto insurance is shutdown-proof. October net income was $828M (−2% y/y).
- **Risk:** The dividend is not free money, because the stock drops by that amount on the ex-date. Auto pricing is softening. A small dividend would disappoint.
- **Plan:** Buy Nov 24–Dec 1 at ≤$233. Stop $218. Sell by Dec 18; target $242.
